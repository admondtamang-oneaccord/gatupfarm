About the farm

A small farm in the hills of Nuwakot, raised by one family.

मे.मे. गडुप पशुपंक्षी तथा फलफूल एग्रो फर्म — established in 2079 BS (2022 AD) — is a registered family-operated agro farm in Salme, Kispang Rural Municipality-4, Nuwakot.

The farm is led by director Birge Bahadur Tamang and works across livestock and poultry husbandry alongside an orchard. It is a small operation with a long view: animals raised with care, and fruit grown to its season.
